# Your TV knows what you're watching / Tu tele sabe qué estás mirando

## Why now
Evergreen. Smart-TV tracking was all over the week of 2026-11-10: a The Verge piece on TV data collection hit 358 points on Hacker News (2026-11-11), "Your TV can see through walls" 279 points, and "Need a TV that isn't a narc" was the top r/privacy post of that week. Most people agreed to ACR once, during setup, and never saw the setting again. The fix is one checkbox, and the official manuals say exactly where it is.

## Notes for the Producer
- Accuracy is 4, not 5, for two reasons: Samsung's official page gives no exact menu path (only "Settings"), and the LG page opened is a 2018 e-manual (Settings > All settings > General > Live Plus). That's why the VO gives the exact path for Roku only and just the setting's **name** for Samsung and LG. Don't add Samsung or LG menu paths from third-party guides.
- "Roku TV" means TVs running Roku's OS (sold under several brands). The Roku steps don't apply to Roku streaming sticks plugged into another brand's TV.
- Don't say "the TV takes screenshots" or "listens to you". Samsung's text says it generates signatures and "does not record or watch any of the content"; Roku says it uses ACR "and other technology". Keep to "turns what's on screen into a signature".
- Samsung's text says ACR runs only if you opted in at setup; Roku prompts the first time you use live TV or an HDMI/AV input. Hence "they ask first, once". Don't claim it's on by default.
- The LG e-manual mentions "interacción con publicidad" (interaction with advertising). The VO doesn't claim what LG collects; an older LG help page said turning Live Plus on consents to viewing-data collection, but that page didn't render today, so it's out.
- Vizio 2017 is a settlement of charges ("to settle charges"), not a court finding. Keep "settle charges".
- No TV on hand: the menu path is a labeled schematic over the real manual page. If the user films their own TV's menu, that becomes a real capture; blur any account name.
- Overlap check: UTP-20261111-02 (Gemini human review settings) is also a "one setting" privacy video; don't schedule them back to back.
