# One invisible text can drop your calls / Un SMS invisible que te corta las llamadas

## Why now
Researcher Mark B published "SMS of Death" on 2026-10-29: a single malformed WAP Push SMS silently crashes Android's phone service and drops active calls, with no tap needed. Google lists it as CVE-2026-58882 (critical, denial of service) in the November 2026 Android Security Bulletin, published 2026-11-02. It was a top post of r/Android this week. Concrete action for every Android viewer: check that the security update date is 2026-11-01 or later.

**Overlap:** this shares the viewer action with UTP-20261105-05 (Android November patch-level explainer). See "Notes for the Producer".

## Sources
- [SMS of Death (researcher write-up)](https://smsofdeath.com/): reported 2026-05-05, public 2026-10-29; WAP Push data SMS, not in inbox; crashes com.android.phone; active calls drop including emergency calls; 1–2 s of no service per message; queued message retried on boot, reboot loop; root cause long→int cast with no range check, NegativeArraySizeException; Android 12 and 13 not patched. Checked 2026-11-06.
- [Android Security Bulletin, November 2026](https://source.android.com/docs/security/bulletin/2026/2026-11-01): published 2026-11-02; CVE-2026-58882 (A-517376969), System, DoS, Critical, AOSP 14, 15, 16, 16-qpr2, 17; listed under the 2026-11-01 patch level. Checked 2026-11-06.
- [CVE-2026-58882 record](https://cveawg.mitre.org/api/cve/CVE-2026-58882): "In decodeWapPdu of WapPushOverSms.java… integer overflow… remote denial of service… User interaction is not needed". Published 2026-11-02. Checked 2026-11-06.
- [AOSP commit 735d967](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/opt/telephony/+/735d9672603fa206c4b43cb668c1aa273db70b97): "Fix remote DoS in decodeWapPdu (b/517376969)", authored 2026-06-15. Checked 2026-11-06.
- [Google Help: check your Android version](https://support.google.com/android/answer/7680439): Settings > About phone > Android version; the security update date is listed there. Checked 2026-11-06.

## Notes for the Producer
- **Never send, build or reproduce the malformed message**, not even to our own phone. Everything about the attack is schematic, labeled as such.
- **Patch date disagreement:** the researcher says the fix shipped in the October 2026 release; Google's CVE record and bulletin list it in November 2026 (2026-11-01 level). The script says "November 1st, 2026 or later", which is safe under both. Don't say "October".
- "Even emergency calls" and the reboot loop come from the researcher's write-up, not from Google. The VO attributes the reboot loop to the researcher; keep it that way.
- Android 12/13: Google's record lists 14–17 as affected; the researcher says 12–13 are affected too and won't be patched. The VO attributes this to the researcher.
- Don't claim it's being exploited in the wild: no source says so.
- **Overlap with UTP-20261105-05** (same viewer action: November patch level). Suggest producing only one: this one has the stronger hook and buzz. If both are produced, -05's two-dates explainer (-01 vs -05) could be a follow-up.
- Menu names vary by phone maker; the ES on-screen labels must match the Spanish UI of the phone captured.
- Pronunciation: "WAP" as a word ("wap"); "CVE" letter by letter.
