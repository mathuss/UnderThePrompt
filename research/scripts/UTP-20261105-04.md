# The sound that gives away where you filmed / El sonido que delata dónde filmaste

## Why now
On 2026-11-02 YouTuber colsto showed that faint aircraft noise in a video's audio, matched against public flight-tracking data (ADS-B), can locate a recording to roughly 100 m, in some cases with under a minute of audio. The video passed 3 million views in three days, hit the top of r/technology and mainstream tech press, and an open-source reimplementation appeared the next day.

## Notes for the Producer
- **Copyright:** don't use colsto's footage or audio. Show his page (title, views) as a source card only. Make our own spectrogram from our own recording.
- **Honesty:** say "about 100 m in his test", never "any video, anywhere". It's not peer-reviewed. Conditions: a known recording time, propeller traffic, ADS-B coverage, usable audio.
- **Safety:** don't demonstrate locating any real person's video. No step-by-step.
- Re-check the view count on production day and round it down ("more than 3 million").
- Possible quick win: the user records 60 s in their own backyard when a plane passes, for the real spectrogram scene.
