# Your Wi-Fi could be someone else's exit door / Tu Wi-Fi puede ser la puerta de salida de otro

## Why now
On 2026-11-04 the threat-intel firm Layer3 Intel reported that, between 2026-10-05 and 2026-11-03, traffic sold by 28 residential proxy services came out of six addresses on the network registered to the Executive Office of the US President, most likely from one person's device. It hit 1,271 points on Hacker News and was covered by CSO Online, Infosecurity Magazine, The Register and others. The useful part for viewers: the same thing happens on ordinary home networks, and Layer3's free page checks your own IP.

## Notes for the Producer
- Don't say "the White House was hacked" or "compromised". Layer3's own wording: proxy software probably on one device; they can't see the device or person. Several outlets went further; we don't.
- "Half in the evening" = the post's "about half between 5 p.m. and midnight Eastern". Keep the inference hedged ("suggests").
- Don't claim the check tool is free forever: the page shows no price and returned a result without sign-in on 2026-11-11. Re-check on production day. Don't show any IP address on screen: blur the producer's IP.
- A negative result only means Layer3 hasn't seen that IP in the last 30 days; IPs get reassigned. Don't promise it proves you're clean.
- FBI PSA is from 2025-06-05 (background, not news). Cite it as "the FBI has warned", no date in VO.
- Don't name specific apps or VPN brands: none of the sources name one for this incident.
- Possible follow-up (not in VO): FBI advice to avoid apps that ask you to turn off Google Play Protect.
