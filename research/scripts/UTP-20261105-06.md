# Make the AI grill you first / Que la IA te interrogue antes de programar

## Why now (evergreen)
mattpocock/skills is one of the most-starred agent-skill collections on GitHub (~314.8k stars) and was #2 on GitHub trending on 2026-11-05 with 2,053 new stars that day. Most people using AI coding agents have never tried skills. "grill-me" is the easiest one to show and the most counter-intuitive: instead of writing code right away, the AI interviews you about your plan.
