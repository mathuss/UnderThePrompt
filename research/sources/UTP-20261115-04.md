## Disagreements and gaps
- BleepingComputer's headline counts 1,235 flaws for November's Patch Tuesday; the MSRC CVRF document holds 1,644 entries (it includes third-party and Chromium CVEs). Neither count is in the script.
- Microsoft says "message file" in one FAQ and "malicious Office file" in another; the script says "message file" and never names an extension.
