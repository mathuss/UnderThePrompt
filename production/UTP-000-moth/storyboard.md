## Shot-size rhythm (alternation check)
CU → M → W → macro → M → CU → W(top) → M → CU → M/CU → W → CU → M → CU/M. There's no run of the same size longer than 2 shots, and the average shot is ~4.4 s. Cutting a beat or two on the energetic lines (5, 8) brings it toward the reference's ~3 s pace.
