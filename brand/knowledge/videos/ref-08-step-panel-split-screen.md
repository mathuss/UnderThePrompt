- Smooth stylised 3D (not faceted). A bearded student in a green sweater at a table in a domed library.
- The AI is a floating orange sphere with a dark face screen: dot eyes, a waveform for a mouth while it talks, a thin orbit ring.

## Why it works
1. **The roast in the first 8 s** makes the viewer like both characters before any tool appears.
2. **The step panel is a promise:** five segments tell the viewer how long this takes and where they are. Nobody leaves at step 4 of 5.
3. **The characters never leave the screen.** The demo and the reactions run together, so there's no dry tutorial stretch.
4. **The limit is a plot beat** (the refusal), and it shows the human staying in control (he presses Submit).
5. **The end card names a person** ("the friend who…"). Shares and comments follow: the comment count is 5–10× the other references.

## What we take
- Split screen: **demo above, characters below**, with a step counter and progress segments. This is the strongest "interactive" device of the five.
- A refusal or limit staged as dialogue.
- A share prompt addressed to a specific kind of friend.

## What we don't take
- The characters, set, serif caption style, the scenario.
- A stylised demo that looks like real product UI. Ours shows real captures inside the panel, or a schematic labelled as one.
- Automating applications or logins on third-party sites (our rules 2 and 6 apply to our own pipeline too).
