# Brand guidelines (draft)

Status: **draft, 2026-11-05.** Open to change. The color palette is being decided in another session (see the `production/design-options/` mockups); this file will link to it once chosen.

## Names
- EN: **Under the Prompt**. ES: **Debajo del Prompt**.
- The promise: *what's really happening under the hood of the tech you use*, from AI to chips, phones, software, security and space, shown with real demos and sources, in under 90 seconds.
- Coverage: all of tech. AI is one beat among several, and the researcher mixes beats.

## Tone

### Shared
- Curious, precise, a little playful. A smart friend explaining something they just tried, not a hype account.
- Show, don't claim. Every number, date and capability comes from a source cited in the script.
- No clickbait the video can't pay off. The hook must be true.
- No "game changer", "insane", "this changes everything", "RIP <product>".
- Explain one idea per video. Jargon is allowed only if it's defined on screen or by an analogy within the same scene.

### EN
- Short, plain sentences. Second person ("you"). Contractions are fine.
- Hook pattern: a concrete surprise or question in under 3 seconds ("This tool reads any program without its source code.").

### ES
- Written natively, not translated. Rioplatense register is fine: "vos", "mirá", "fijate", "probalo".
- Keep common English tech terms when that's how people say them (prompt, MCP, open source, benchmark); explain them the first time.
- Avoid Spain-only or overly Mexican slang so it reads well across LatAm.
- Hook pattern: same idea as EN, rewritten for rhythm ("Claude puede leer cualquier programa… sin el código fuente.").

## Structure of a script (60–90 s)
1. **Hook** (0–3 s): the surprise.
2. **Context** (3–15 s): what it is and why now.
3. **How it works / demo** (15–60 s): real UI or terminal, one visual action per block.
4. **Limits / honesty** (a few seconds): what it can't do, cost, availability.
5. **Takeaway** (one line).
6. **CTA** (last 3–5 s).

On-screen text: **max 4 words per scene** (subtitles aside).

## CTA lines
EN (rotate):
- "Follow Under the Prompt for what's really under the hood of tech."
- "Follow for the tech news that actually matters."
- "Sources in the description. Follow for more."

ES (rotar):
- "Seguí a Debajo del Prompt para ver qué hay debajo de la tecnología."
- "Seguinos para las noticias de tecnología que importan de verdad."
- "Fuentes en la descripción. Seguinos para más."

## Typography (already used in the Remotion template)
- **Inter** (400 / 600 / 800): titles, captions, UI text.
- **JetBrains Mono**: code, terminal output, file names, tool names, IDs.
- Both are loaded from `@remotion/google-fonts` (SIL Open Font License).

## Do / don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Real screen captures, or schematic animations labeled as such | Fake UIs, invented logos, AI-generated "screenshots" |
| Cite primary sources (official blog, docs, repo, paper) | Cite only a tweet or an aggregator |
| Say what it can't do | Promise results the demo doesn't show |
| No music, or CC0 with license saved | Copyrighted music |
